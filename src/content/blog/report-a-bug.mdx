Something looks wrong? Click the **bug** button next to the Settings gear in the sidebar (on macOS also **Help → Report a Bug…**, or the button in **About**).

1. Write a title and what happened — what you did, what you expected, what you saw instead.
2. Add an e-mail or a GitHub name if you would like us to ask back. It is optional.
3. Press **Send report**.

Files sends it to our website, which files it as an issue on [github.com/FileForce/files](https://github.com/FileForce/files/issues). You get the link right away, and you do not need a GitHub account.

## What is sent

- Your title and description, and the contact if you gave one.
- The version of Files and your system, e.g. *macOS 26.1 · aarch64*.
- **Only if you tick the box:** the last 60 events from the Console — names of commands and files Files worked with, never their contents.

<Callout title="Good to know">
Issues are public, so leave passwords and private paths out. If the website cannot take a report — offline, say — the issue opens in your browser already filled in, ready to submit.
</Callout>

- **SVG thumbnails, cached:** an SVG card used to be drawn at the file's own size — a 1024 px SVG with filters cost ~290 ms and 4 MB per card. Now each SVG is rasterized once to a 320 px PNG in the background thumbnail worker (transparency kept) and cached. A screen of 30 heavy SVGs fills in ~0.9 s the first time without blocking the window, then redraws in ~18 ms. How we measured: [the SVG stress test](/blog/svg-stress-test).
- **Windows and Linux builds:** 0.1.25 shipped for macOS only — a macOS-only helper for app icons broke the other builds. Fixed; 0.1.26 is out on all three.

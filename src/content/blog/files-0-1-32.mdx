- **Lock:** Settings → General. Files hides every window when it starts, after a while without use, or when the computer locks its screen or sleeps. It opens with the system's own check — Touch ID or the Mac's password, Windows Hello, your password on Linux. Files keeps no password of its own.
- **Frame rate** in the MEDIA section of the preview: `60 fps`, `29.94 fps`. OBS and screen recordings had none, because they keep their timing in pieces of the file. Files now reads it there.
- **Sidebar from Music and Mail:** a favorite, disk, tag or the Trash opens in the tab that already shows it, or in a new one. Before, nothing happened.

---
title: Middle-click opens and closes tabs
date: 2026-11-03
tag: tip
summary: Click a folder with the mouse wheel to open it in a new tab; click a tab with the wheel to close it.
cover: /blog/list.png
coverAlt: A folder listed in Files
---

Just like in a browser:

- **Middle-click a folder** — in the list, the sidebar or the breadcrumbs — and it opens in a new tab, leaving the current one where it is.
- **Middle-click a tab** to close it.

The same is in the context menu as **Open in New Tab**. Tabs can be dragged to reorder them, and their menu shows each shortcut in grey, so you learn them as you go.

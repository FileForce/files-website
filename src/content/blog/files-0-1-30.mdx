- **Spatial audio:** Settings → Extensions → Music. Stereo becomes a virtual 5.1 room around your head, made in Files — the same on macOS, Windows and Linux. Off, **With headphones**, or Always. The whole story: [Spatial audio, inside a file manager](/blog/spatial-audio).
- **Follow my head** (macOS, AirPods and Beats): turn your head, and the sound stays where it was.
- **Speakers:** Headphones, 2.1, 3.1, 5.1 or 7.1, with a test tone per speaker. With surround speakers, Music spreads stereo over them: voice to the centre, lows to the subwoofer, ambience behind.
- **Equalizer** in the Music settings, heard in every mode.
- **SSH with a key:** type its path, choose it, or pick one found in `~/.ssh`. The password field is the key's passphrase, kept in the keychain.

Settings usually opens over the window and closes when you are done. When you want to try things — a theme, a shortcut, how an extension behaves — keep it in a tab instead:

- Press **Open in tab** in the Settings header. Edits you have not saved yet come along.
- Or **middle-click the gear** in the sidebar, the same way a middle-click opens a folder in a new tab.

The tab is a normal tab: drag it, pin it, close it with a middle-click. **Save** applies and keeps it open, **Cancel** throws away unsaved edits. While it is open, <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>,</Kbd> takes you to it instead of opening a second Settings.

<Callout>
Everything written in Settings can be selected and copied now — handy for pasting a path or an explanation into a note or a bug report.
</Callout>

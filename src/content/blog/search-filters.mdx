The search box understands filters. Combine them with any words:

```text
kind:image size:>10mb modified:<7d
```

That finds pictures bigger than 10 MB changed in the last week.

| Filter | Example | Finds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kind:` | `kind:video` | image, video, audio, document, code, archive, folder |
| `size:` | `size:>1gb`, `size:<100kb` | bigger or smaller than |
| `modified:` | `modified:<7d`, `modified:>1y` | changed within, or longer ago than |

<Callout title="Tip">
Press <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>F</Kbd> (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>F</Kbd> on Windows and Linux) to jump to the box, and <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> to search everywhere, not only in this folder. Extensions add their own filters, like `git:` — see them all in Settings → Extensions.
</Callout>

- **Enhance to FLAC:** right-click an MP3 (or M4A, AAC, Ogg) → Quick Actions → **Enhance to FLAC…**. A harmonic exciter rebuilds the highs MP3 cuts off, quiet tracks are lifted towards −14 LUFS without clipping, and the result is 24-bit FLAC with its tags and cover. Low, Medium or High; stereo or 5.1. About a second per 4-minute track.
- **Upmix to 5.1 Surround:** stereo spread over six channels — voice in the centre, lows in the subwoofer, ambience behind.
- **Enhanced streaming:** Settings → Extensions → Music → **Enhance streaming**. Yandex Music sends MP3; Files now enhances it *while it downloads*, and the first sound arrives in about 0.1 s. Played again, the track comes from a FLAC cache.
- **New files show up:** a file made by another program — an agent, a terminal, a download — appears in the open folder within a second. Before, it waited until Files itself changed something.

All of it is built into Files: no ffmpeg to install. The whole story: [Making MP3 sound better, inside a file manager](/blog/mp3-to-flac-enhance).

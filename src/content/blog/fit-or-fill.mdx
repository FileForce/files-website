A square photo in a wide preview has two honest options: show all of it with bands at the sides, or fill the frame and cut the top and bottom. Files used to pick the second for you. Now it shows **the whole picture** by default — in the preview panel and in Quick Look.

Next to the picture there is a small **Fit ⇄ Fill** button:

- **Fit** — the whole picture, nothing cut off. Best for photos you are checking: faces, documents, screenshots.
- **Fill** — the frame filled, edges trimmed. Best when you browse for mood and want big, even previews.

Your choice is remembered for both places. The grid in the Expressive view keeps filling its cards, so the tiles stay neat.

<Callout title="Tip">
Press <Kbd>Space</Kbd> for Quick Look — the bigger view — and the same button is at its bottom.
</Callout>
